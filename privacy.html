Privacy policy

Roi Personal Assistant is a private application operated by Roi for personal use. Last updated: October 3, 2026.

Google data accessed and its purpose

With the operator's authorization, the assistant accesses Google Drive file metadata and file contents to find, read, summarize, and organize notes. A separate writer authorization allows it to create and update assistant-generated Drive files. Read-only Calendar authorization allows it to retrieve calendar event information.

Processing and sharing

Information selected for assistant tasks may be sent to configured AI services, currently including OpenAI, to generate responses and summaries. Responses may appear in the operator's connected Slack or Telegram accounts. These services process information under their own applicable terms, privacy policies, and account settings.

The assistant does not sell Google data or use it for advertising. These public informational pages contain no private Drive files, calendar events, or Google credentials.

Storage and security

Retrieved information, derived knowledge notes, and conversation history may be stored on the operator's server, in configured Google Drive output folders, and in operator-managed backups. OAuth credentials are stored in protected server configuration and are not published on this website.

Retention, deletion, and revocation

Stored records remain until the operator deletes them. The operator can remove assistant access through Google Account permissions at any time. Revoking access stops future authorized API access but does not automatically delete previously stored notes, conversations, output files, or backups; those copies must be removed separately.

Contact and changes

Contact roihezkiyahu@gmail.com about access or deletion. This policy will be updated when the application's data practices change.
