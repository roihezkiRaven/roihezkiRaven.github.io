Roi Personal Assistant

A private assistant operated by Roi for personal use. It helps search and summarize authorized Google Drive notes, organize a personal knowledge library, and read Google Calendar events.

The Drive reader accesses authorized files for retrieval and summarization. The Drive writer creates and updates assistant-generated files within the configured output folders. Calendar access is read-only.

Selected information may be processed by configured AI providers and displayed in the operator's connected messaging accounts. Google access requires the operator's explicit authorization.

This website contains information about the assistant. It does not provide public access to the assistant or to private Google data.
